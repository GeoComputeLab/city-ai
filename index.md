# CITY-AI: CyberTraining for AI-Enabled Intelligent Urban Informatics and Smart City Innovation

The CITY-AI project, funded by the National Science Foundation’s Office of Advanced Cyberinfrastructure, aims to introduce advanced cyberinfrastructure and artificial intelligence to urban informatics communities by developing a cyberinfrastructure-enabled urban informatics curriculum. CITY-AI will deliver educational materials, workshops, webinars, and summer schools to support research, education, and workforce development.
