**Dr. Carol Song** is a Co-Principal Investigator (Co-PI) for CITY-AI and Chief Scientist at the Rosen Center for Advanced Computing (RCAC) at Purdue University.

She is the PI for NSF Anvil (OAC-2005632), a national composable advanced computational resource for science and engineering. She has decades of experience leading national-scale cyberinfrastructure, high-performance computing, and scientific gateway projects.
