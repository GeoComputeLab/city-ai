**Dr. JaeWoo Shin** is a Research Scientist in the Scientific Solutions Group at the Rosen Center for Advanced Computing (RCAC) at Purdue University.

His research focuses on efficient indexing techniques for update-intensive data workloads, streaming data platforms (StreamCI), and cyberinfrastructure development for advanced scientific applications.
